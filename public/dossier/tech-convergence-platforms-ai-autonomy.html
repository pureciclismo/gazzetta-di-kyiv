# Enterprise Intelligence Consolidation --- ## Structural Thesis The enterprise software landscape is being fundamentally restructured by the integration of AI into every layer of the stack — cloud infrastructure, data platforms, security, CRM, and productivity. This is not "digital transformation" (the 2015 consulting slogan). This is the consolidation of enterprise intelligence into a small number of platform monopolies that own the data, the compute, and the AI models. The structural dynamic is self-reinforcing. Large language models improve with scale — more data, more compute, better performance. The enterprises that already own the cloud infrastructure (Microsoft Azure, Amazon AWS, Google Cloud) are the only entities capable of training frontier models. The enterprises that already own the enterprise software distribution channels (Microsoft 365, Salesforce, ServiceNow) are the only entities capable of monetizing AI at scale. The result is a consolidation flywheel that concentrates enterprise value in 3-5 platform companies. The media covers this as a "tech stock" story — buying QQQ as a growth trade. The structural reality is that enterprise AI is a winner-take-most platform war where the incumbents have insurmountable advantages. The market is mispricing the longevity of this consolidation because it is still treating AI as a cyclical growth theme rather than a permanent restructuring of the enterprise software industry.
